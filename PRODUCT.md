# Sawa Coffee House digital menu

**Who:** Sawa (سوا, "together"), a new premium coffee house in Egypt. Coffee, matcha, shakes, juices, bakery and desserts. Instagram @sawacoffeeofficial.
**Users:** guests on their phones, at a table (QR) or ordering takeaway.
**Surface mode:** Read first: browse the menu and order at the cashier. Ordering from the phone is a switch in INBYTE Admin, off for now.
**Ordering (when switched on):** straight to Sawa's INBYTE Café cashier through the INBYTE platform (`/api/public/v1/tenants/sawa`). The cashier pulls the order (outbound connector) and it appears in «الطلبات الواردة». WhatsApp ordering can be added later.
**Language:** Egyptian Arabic, Western digits.
